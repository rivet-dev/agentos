---
title: "Vercel Eve"
description: "Use the fixed agentOS actor as Vercel Eve's VM backend."
---

Eve owns its application runtime; agentOS supplies the isolated VM and durable
workspace.

<CodeSnippet file="examples/vercel-eve/agent/sandbox.ts" />

`agentOSBackend({ createInput, clientConfig })` connects to the fixed actor.
Each Eve key maps to a stable actor key. Shutdown stops processes opened by the
adapter and disconnects; it does not destroy durable actor state.

Use `agentOSCoreBackend` when a trusted embedding needs host-backed mounts or
bindings.
